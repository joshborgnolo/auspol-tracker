What Worries the World?

Ipsos’ What Worries the World survey tracks public
opinion on the most important social and political
issues across 30 countries today, drawing on over
ten years of data to place the latest scores in context.

This preview report presents the top concerns around the
world, alongside whether people think things in their country
are heading in the right or wrong direction.
For the full report, please contact Joseph.Nadler@Ipsos.com.
