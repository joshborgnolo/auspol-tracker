October-December 2013 and is based on a total of 4564 interviews among voters. Individual
state sample bases range from 554 to 1352 voters. Note: sample size in Tasmania is too small for a
meaningful separate analysis, however Tasmania is included in total figures. Northern Territory
is excluded. Sampling error ranges from plus or minus 1.5 percentage points on the total sample
