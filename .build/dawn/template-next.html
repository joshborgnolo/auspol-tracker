How these dates have done

Each date comes from the pollster’s own record: the median gap between its last releases, the weekday it keeps, and the hours its releases have come out, all Sydney time. A date that passes with no poll on record shows as late for two days, then counts as skipped and the next date takes over; a skip the pollster confirms moves it on at once. A monthly pollster with no fixed day gets the span of days it has used. Nobody publishes between 23 December and 8 January, so a date there gives way to the window pollsters returned across last summer, 9 January to 1 February. Direction and issues surveys are listed, never counted as polls. The dates are the site’s, the same ones its Latest and next polls table shows.
