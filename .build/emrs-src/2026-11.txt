METHODOLOGY

•   For the Omnibus surveys done by EMRS, 1,000 Tasmanians are polled each quarter via an interviewer led questionnaire conducted
    with Computer Assisted Telephone Interviewing (CATI) and online via Computer Assisted Web Interviewing (CAWI).

•   Quotas are used to balance the age, gender and location of respondents to roughly align with the ABS Census, currently based on
    the 2021 census data. The target population was based on 445,067 Tasmanian residents aged 18 years and over (source: ABS
    Census, 2021). In total, a sample of n=1,000 completed the survey, yielding overall results accurate to within ±3.10 percentage
    points at the 95% confidence level.

•   Weighting is then applied to the data based on age group, gender, federal electorate, highest education achieved and past vote
    (Tasmanian House of Assembly) to ensure that balance and provide the most accurate results possible.

•   The most recent Omnibus survey was conducted from 11-12 November 2026 utilising EMRS’ in-house call centre and online panel,
    ensuring high levels of quality control and that the target sample sizes, and their distribution regionally, were achieved or closely
    achieved with low and statistically acceptable weightings.

•   The sample is robust and reliable. Cross-tab analysis was carried out to ensure the robustness of the findings.

•   Statistically significant results are in bold and highlighted in a dark shade of green for results that are significantly higher or red for
    results that is significantly lower at the 95% confidence interval.



This survey was conducted to the market and social research ISO20252:2019 standard.
