 FORMAT
For voting intention questions, all respondents were presented with the final list of nominated candidates in ballot
paper order for their electorate.

A sample electorate question is set out below

Q. If a Federal Election for the Adelaide electorate was held today, which one of the following candidates would
you vote for?

Answers (Candidates in ballot paper order with Don't Know at end)

Lionel Pengilley (Animal Justice Party)
Steve Georganas (Australian Labor Party)
Steve Marks (Trumpet of Patriots)
Mat Monti (The Greens)
Matthew McMillan (FUSION | Planet Rescue | Whistleblower Protection | Innovation)
Riley Size (Pauline Hanson's One Nation)
Amy Grantham (Liberal)
Don’t know

If a respondent chose don’t know they were asked a follow up leaner question in the following terms with the same
answer options.

Q. Being undecided, if a Federal Election for the Adelaide electorate was held today which of the following would
you have a slight leaning towards?
