METHODOLOGY DISCLOSURE STATEMENT

 Project name                  Mood of the Nation (Wave 23)

 Project reference             NGR 2203003



 Research dates:               29th October – 3rd November 2025
 Target population:            The target population for the research was Australian residents
                               (all states and territories) aged 18+.
 Research methodology:         The research comprised of a 20-minute self-complete online
                               survey with n=1,208 participants, sourced from an ISO accredited
                               market research panel.


 Weighting approach:           The data was weighted using ABS Census population statistics on
                               age, gender, location and occupation to match the estimated
                               population of all Australian residents aged 18+. The total effective
                               sample size after weighting was approximately 79%, equating to a
                               margin of error of approximately +-3.2% (at the 95% confidence
                               interval).
 Research questions:           A combination of single choice, multiple choice and open-ended
                               (text response) questions were used in the research. All questions
                               and response options were displayed in full during interviews.


 This research was conducted in accordance with the Australian Polling Council Quality Mark
 standards which can be viewed here: https://www.australianpollingcouncil.com/
