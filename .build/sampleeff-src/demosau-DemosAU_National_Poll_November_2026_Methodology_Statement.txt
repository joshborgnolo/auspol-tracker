Methodology Statement
  DEMOSAU NATIONAL POLL FOR CAPITAL BRIEF, NOVEMBER 2026

  Research company                                        DemosAU

  Client commissioning the research                       Capital Brief

  Fieldwork dates                                         18–22 November 2026

  Mode of data collection                                 Internet panels

  Target population                                       Australian residents 18+ eligible to vote at elections

  Sample size                                             1,464

  Is voting intention published?                          Yes, in this report

  Effective sample size after weighting applied           711

  Margin of error associated with effective sample size   ±3.7%

  Variables used in weighting                             Age x gender, education, personal income, residential tenure, language, state x region, 2025 Federal Vote, Left-Right
                                                          Self ID.

  Weighting method used                                   Rim Weighting

  randomisation

  Source of sample                                        Internet panels

  How were undecided voters handled?                      Initial undecided voters were asked a leaner question. Remaining undecided voters were excluded from final totals.
