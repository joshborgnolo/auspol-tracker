Long Methodology Disclosure Statement
Research conducted by: Fox & Hedgehog
Client commissioning research: Self-initiated
Fieldwork dates: 17-19 February 2026
Mode of data collection: Recruited from online research panels
Target population: Eligible South Australian voters (18+, Australian Citizens)
Sample Size: 1,625
Effective sample size post-weighting: 1068
Margin of error: 3.0% (larger for subgroups)
Weighting variables and method: RIM Weighting. Age x Gender x Education, Region, Income, State x 2025
Past Vote (Federal). Sourced from the Australian Bureau of Statistics and the Australian Electoral
Commission.
Position of voting intention questions: After demographics
3PP & TPP preference distribution method: Respondent allocated
   Three-party preferred (3PP): Respondents who selected Greens, Independent or Other as their first
   preference were asked which of Labor, the Coalition or One Nation they would prefer in a three-way
   contest. These responses were then allocated to Labor, the Coalition or One Nation and added to their
   respective primary votes to produce the 3PP result.
   Two-party preferred (2PP) match-ups: Separate questions were asked for each hypothetical contest;
   Labor vs the Coalition, Labor vs One Nation, and the Coalition vs One Nation. For each match-up,
   respondents whose first preference was not one of the two parties were allocated according to their
   stated preference.
Handling of undecided voters: Voters were provided the option to select “don’t know”. Voters who selected
“don’t know” were given a leaner question, with the same options provided. Voters who expressed “don’t
know” to both questions (comprising 4% of respondents) were excluded from voter intention related figures,
but included for other questions.
Australian Polling Council Compliant: Yes
