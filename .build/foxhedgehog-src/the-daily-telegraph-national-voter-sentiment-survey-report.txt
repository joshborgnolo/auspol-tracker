RESEARCH METHODOLOGY STATEMENT

Research conducted by: Fox & Hedgehog
Client commissioning research: Self-initiated
Fieldwork dates: 5 January - 6 January 2026
Mode of data collection: Recruited from online research panels
Target population: Eligible Australian voters (18+, Australian Citizens)
Sample Size: 1,608
Effective sample size post-weighting: 1,038
Margin of error: 3.0% (larger for subgroups)
Weighting variables and method: RIM weighting. Age x Gender x Education, Income, Region, State x 2025 Past Vote
3PP and TPP preference distribution method: Respondent allocated.
   Three-party preferred (3PP): Respondents who selected Greens, Independent or Other as their first preference were asked
   which of Labor, the Coalition or One Nation they would prefer in a three-way contest. These responses were then allocated
   to Labor, the Coalition or One Nation and added to their respective primary votes to produce the 3PP result.
   Two-party preferred (2PP) match-ups: Separate questions were asked for each hypothetical contest; Labor vs Coalition,
   Labor vs One Nation, and Coalition vs One Nation. For each match-up, respondents whose first preference was not one of
   the two parties were allocated according to their stated preference.
