EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
 1.   Six in ten (60%) think Australia is heading in the wrong direction, up 7pts from this
      time last year.

 2.   65% of Australians think the economic outlook over the next 12 months will
      worsen, while 43% think their personal finances will worsen.

 3.   The cost of living (69%), housing (40%), and immigration (27%) are the top priority
      issues among Australian voters at this time. The proportion of those citing fuel as
      their primary cost of living concern has risen from 3% to 14% since December.

 4.   Labor leads the primary vote share on 28%, followed by One nation (26%), and the
      Coalition (24%). Labor leads the coalition 51% to 49% on a TPP basis, and One
      Nation 52% to 48%.

 5.   Albanese leads Angus Taylor as preferred Prime Minister 41% to 32%, and leads
      Hanson 44% to 39%.

 6.   Hanson (Net +4) and One Nation (+9) have the highest approval rating of political
      figures and parties tested, with Hanson 1pt ahead of Liberal leader Taylor (+3), and
      26pts ahead of Albanese on -22.

 7.   Coalition defectors to One Nation are from across the spectrum, but
      disproportionately identify as working class or lower middle class.                     S
 8.   Nearly half (49%) of One Nation supporters want to see Australian institutions and      P
      laws completely rebuilt, with a disproportionately larger share among those who
      identify as working or lower middle class.                                              E
 9.   Three quarters of Australians think wealth inequality is growing, and almost half       C
      (47%) say it is growing a lot. Among those who think wealth inequality is growing a
      lot, a disproportionate 44% want to see major institutional overhaul.                   T
