AUSTRALIAN POLITICAL UPDATE

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
 1.   Over six in ten (64%) Australians think the country is heading in the wrong
      direction at this time , compared with just one in five (21%) who think it is heading
      in the right direction.

 2.   Taxation and Government spending is up 7pts from July in national issue salience
      (21%), while Crime is up 5pts to 29%, now the third most important issue in
      Australia.

 3.   A clear majority (61%) of Australians expect the overall economic situation to
      deteriorate over the next year.

 4.   One Nation leads the Primary Vote share on 28%, followed by Labor on 25%, and
      the Coalition on 24%.

 5.   Labor’s vote share has dramatically softened since July, up 13pts, with now four in
      ten Labor voters soft in their commitment, similar to Coalition and Greens
      supporters.

 6.   Approval ratings for Federal Labor and associated figures have fallen sharply
      since July, the party down Net 14pts to -18, Albanese down to -25, and Chalmers to
      -19.

 7.   Albanese leads as Australia’s preferred Prime Minister (34%), followed by Pauline       S
      Hanson (30%) and Angus Taylor (22%).
