EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
 1.   Just under six in ten Australians think the country I heading in the wrong
      direction currently (57%), compared to just one in three (29%) who think it is
      heading in the right direction.

 2.   Cost of living (65%) remains Australians top priority issue, followed by housing
      (39%), and immigration (30%). Issue salience is relatively unchanged year over
      year, with immigration up most (+6pts since July 2025).

 3.   Labor leads on primary vote share (28%), followed closely by One Nation (26%),
      then the Coalition(23%).

 4.   Labor leads on a TPP to 51% to 49% vs the Coalition and 54% to 46% vs One
      Nation.

 5.   One in four Australians are ‘soft’ in their vote intention, with an increasing
      proportion of soft voters among One Nation and the Greens compared to prior
      months.

 6.   One Nation (+2) and Hanson (-1) have the highest approval ratings of tested
      figures, albeit down since April. While Albanese (-14) and Labor (-4) have
      recovered by 8 and 10pts respectively since April.                                 S
 7.   Angus Taylor’s approval rating has somewhat weakened since coming into the         P
      party’s leadership, down 10pts since April to -7.
