QUEENSLAND
====================================================================

LEGISLATIVE ASSEMBLY
====================================================================

THE FIFTY-FIFTH PARLIAMENT
ELECTED 31 JANUARY 2015
====================================================================
The Fifty-Fifth Parliament was opened on 24 March 2015 and 
dissolved on 29 October 2017.

Sources
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Election Commission of Queensland, Queensland Parliament website

Summary
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Annastacia Palaszczuk led Labor back into government at this 
election after only one term in opposition, defeating Campbell 
Newman's Liberal National government. Labor entered the campaign 
holding nine seats, and gained 37, to finish with 44, one short of a 
majority. Palaszczuk formed a minority government with the support 
of the independent member Peter Wellington, who became Speaker.

Newman's defeat was attributed to his government's austerity 
policies, particularly its heavy cuts to the public service, and to 
his own autocratic and confrontational style. He was defeated at 
Ashgrove, only the second Queensland Premier to lose his seat. Three 
of his ministers and six assistant ministers were also defeated. 

Newman was succeeded as Liberal National leader by Lawrence 
Springborg, who had already been Leader of the Opposition twice and 
had lost three elections to Labor. In May 2016 Springborg was 
deposed and succeeded by Tim Nicholls.

Of the 34 Labor members defeated in 2012, only six were re-elected 
at this election: Cameron Dick, Di Farmer, Grace Grace, Stirling 
Hinchliffe, Kate Jones and Mark Ryan. Palaszczuk's ministry included 
five members newly elected to Parliament.
