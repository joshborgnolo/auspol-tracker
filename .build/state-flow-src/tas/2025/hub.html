2025 State election




  • House of Assembly
  • Current: 2025 State election











A general election for the House of Assembly was held on Saturday 19 July 2025.

Tasmania has five divisions: Bass, Braddon, Clark, Franklin and Lyons. Seven members will be elected to represent each division for a term of up to four years.

Voting is compulsory.

Ways to vote

 Polling closed at 6 pm Saturday 19 July 2025.

You can vote in person at a polling place on polling day. If you can't do this, there will be early and alternative voting options available.

Learn more about ways to vote  









The candidates

Nominations have closed. Candidates for the 2025 State election were announced on Friday 27 June at 12 noon. Details were published online at 12:05 pm.

Election night tally room

The TEC will once again host a tally room on polling night, in the Federation Ballroom at the Hotel Grand Chancellor, 1 Davey St, Hobart. This event is open to the public, doors open at 6 pm. It is anticipated that first results from polling places will be available from around 6:45 pm.

It's a decision we make together

Featuring local faces in familiar places, our new election awareness campaign captures the experience of voters across Tasmania. Thank you to the community members who collaborated with us to shine a light on the shared experience of election day, and celebrate democracy in action. Because… it’s a decision we make, together.


Enrolment

To vote in this State election, you must be correctly enrolled before 6 pm Wednesday 18 June 2025.

Further information is available to assist you if you need to enrol to vote or update your enrolment for future elections.
