What happens at House of Assembly elections

The election begins with the enrolment period and the issue of the Writs of election.

The election period continues with the nomination of candidates and the announcement of nominations.

Once the candidates are known, early voting, polling day and election night take place.

After polling day results are finalised with the receipt of pre-poll and postal votes, the Hare-Clark scrutiny and the declaration of the poll.
