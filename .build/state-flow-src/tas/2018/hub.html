Tallyroom 2018

We are proud to be continuing the tradition of an election Tallyroom for the 2018 State Election. Members of the public are invited to attend.

Some details:

  • Location: Federation Ballroom, Hotel Grand Chancellor, Davy St Hobart
  • Time: Tallyroom is open to the public from 6pm.
  • Facilities: Public bar serving some wine and soft drinks, lift access.
  • Security: As is becoming common practice now days, wanding and bag checks will be in place.
