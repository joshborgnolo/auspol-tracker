Vote Compass data suggests priorities have changed since 2022

Respondents say cost of living is the key issue ahead of this year's election, whereas climate change was the most important issue for respondents in 2022.

The 2022 data is based on 471,491 responses to Vote Compass and the 2025 data based on 254,376 responses. There is a modelled error estimate of +/-0.5%. Totals may not sum to 100 due to rounding.
