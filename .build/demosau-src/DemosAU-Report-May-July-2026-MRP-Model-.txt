The electorate vote shares indicated by the DemosAU MRP should be considered with a confidence interval at
95% probability of +/- 5%. To reflect this uncertainty, each seat is classified based on a calculated probability of
victory by the leading party. the strength of the result in the following three categories;

In Doubt: Less than 70% probability of victory.
Lean: 70-95% probability of victory. .
Firm: Greater than 95% probability of victory.

The seat ranges for each party or grouping are based on a Monte Carlo probability analysis of 20,000
simulations. The seat ranges reflect a 95% confidence interval.

A Note on Preferences
 To determine an estimated electorate two-candidate preferred result, a mixture of previous 2025 election preference data
 and respondent allocated preference data is used.

 Respondent allocated preference flow data is obtained by using the preferences indicated by over 9000 respondents
 nation-wide in the polling for this MRP over the past 2 months. The respondent allocated preference were obtained from
 respondents completely ranking the 5 voting options (ALP, L/NP, Grn, ONP, Oth).

 Even at this scale we are only using preference flows informed by respondent data in relation to ALP-ONP, L/NP-ONP, Grn-
 ONP, Oth-ONP TCP contests and the ON preferences in ALP-L/NP contests. In all other cases our preference flow
 assumptions are informed by previous 2025 election preferences as before.

 The final two in each electorate are determined by applying three candidate preferred flows to party/groups which
 finished third or better on the primary vote. The 3CP flows are estimated using a mixture of previous 2025 election
 preference data and respondent allocated preference data.

 Please see the following page for an overview of TCP respondent allocated preference flows used for this model.
