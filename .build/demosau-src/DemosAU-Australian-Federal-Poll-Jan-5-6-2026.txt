NOTE ON LABOR/ONP TWO PARTY PREFERRED ESTIMATE

As One Nation is tied for second with the Coalition nationally, it is necessary to consider a nation-
wide ALP/ONP TPP estimate. Unlike the standard Labor v Coalition TPP, full nation-wide preference
data is not available for this estimate.

The preference assumptions used for an ALP v ONP TPP are;
   Coalition: ONP 82.9% ALP 17.1% (observed Hunter 2025 preference flow)
   Greens: ALP 88.2% ONP 11.8% (ALP v LNP Nationwide Preference Flow, with ONP replacing LNP)
   Others: 50%-50% (As the composition of Others is not known, a 50-50 preference split has been
   adopted.)

Give the absence of full nation-wide preference data, a Labor/ONP TPP estimate should be treated
with caution.                                                                                    6
