Unprompted concerns and priorities
Cost of living concerns have stabilised, being mentioned by 64%. Concerns about housing affordability (37%,) crime (20%) and healthcare (13%)
remain relatively steady, with crime significantly more likely to be a concern in Victoria and Queensland. Mentions of both climate change (13%)
and inflation (6%) have risen slightly, while immigration & population mentions have fallen back to 13% after reaching a record high in September.
