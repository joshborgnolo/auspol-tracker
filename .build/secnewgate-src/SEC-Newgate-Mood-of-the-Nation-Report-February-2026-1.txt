Unprompted concerns and priorities
Cost of living remains the most mentioned issue of concern (mentioned by 61%). Housing affordability (33%) and crime (17%) round out the top
three mentions, although mentions of both have marginally eased since late 2025. Immigration and population, however, is mentioned by 16%,
again representing a high-water mark for this issue.

Australia’s national priorities (top 12 out of 36)

investing in healthcare (63%) and reducing crime and antisocial behaviour (61%), although the latter’s perceived importance has receded slightly
since November. Strengthening the response to racial and religious violence has also risen a little to 48%, representing a new high.
