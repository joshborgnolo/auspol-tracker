Unprompted concerns and priorities
Cost of living remains the most commonly mentioned concern (68%), ahead of housing affordability (32%). This is followed by crime, at its highest
level since late 2025 (20%), and immigration & population (17%), which has reached a new record high. Unprompted mentions of crime are also
much higher in Victoria (32%) than is the case elsewhere in Australia.

Perceived government performance
