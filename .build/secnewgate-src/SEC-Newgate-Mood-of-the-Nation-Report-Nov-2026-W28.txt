Unprompted concerns and priorities
Cost of living remains the leading concern (71%), at its highest level in about 18 months. This is followed by housing affordability (25%), with
mentions of this falling since September. Mentions of immigration and healthcare have fallen, while interest rates, unemployment and petrol prices
have recorded increases since September.

importance of managing risks associated with AI has also risen to a new high of 53%.
