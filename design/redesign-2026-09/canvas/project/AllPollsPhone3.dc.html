A pollster’s lean is its average gap to the other pollsters polling within four weeks of it, all read through the same preference flows, with recent polls counting most. One with few polls is pulled towards zero until its record builds. Because every poll uses the same flows, a lean comes from a pollster’s primary votes, not from how it allocates preferences.
