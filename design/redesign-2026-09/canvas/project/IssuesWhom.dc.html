The issues What voters say matters most, and who they think is best on it · Resolve, Ipsos, RedBridge and YouGov, last six weeks

The cost of living comes first for everyone. What comes second divides them.

For One Nation voters it is immigration: 63% put it in their top three, against 29% of all voters. For Greens voters it is housing (55%), and for Labor voters, health (51%).
