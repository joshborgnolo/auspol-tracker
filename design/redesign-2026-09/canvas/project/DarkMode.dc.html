Dark mode The site’s variables, and the ones this redesign adds

Every new colour needs a dark twin

The site already switches its colours through variables when a reader picks dark mode. The redesign adds tints, ramps and text-on-fill colours. Their values in each theme are below, then the new charts drawn in dark. The other mock-ups use close hex values; the port should use these variables.
