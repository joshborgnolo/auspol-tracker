About one voter in twenty is undecided. A quarter of the rest could still switch.

Neither share has moved significantly since the 2025 election. Undecided voters have mostly run between 5% and 7% in Roy Morgan’s weekly polls; the latest pooled figure, 4.4%, is lower mainly because of a single 1% reading.

Mid-2025 (June–October 2025) Now (June–August 2026) Change in bold: significant

RedBridge asks voters how firm their choice is, a different question from Resolve’s in All voters, so the two views don’t compare directly. Among the parties, only the Coalition’s fall is significant. The share of all voters calling their vote solid has also fallen significantly, from 52.3% to 48.7%.
