Both major parties are at record lows for this point in a term

Sixteen months after the 2025 election, Labor’s primary vote is the lowest of any government at the same point since 1972, and the Coalition’s the lowest of any opposition. After preferences, though, Labor’s 51.2% sits in the middle half of past governments.

After preferences, Labor is on a par with governments that went on to be re-elected

Its 51.2% against One Nation is 1.9 points above the average government 16 months in. Governments later re-elected averaged 50.6% at this point; the six turned out averaged 46.3%.
