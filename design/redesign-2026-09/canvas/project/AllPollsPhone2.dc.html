The polls disagree on how the right’s vote splits, not on its size

Polls of One Nation’s vote typically sit 2.3 points from the trend through them, and polls of the Coalition’s 2.1: about half as much again as sampling error alone would put them. Add the two parties together, though, and their combined vote varies only as much as chance allows, as Labor’s does.
