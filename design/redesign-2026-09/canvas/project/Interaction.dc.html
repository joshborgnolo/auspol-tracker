Interaction states Hover on desktop, tap on touch screens

Every chart keeps its trail back to the polls

The redesign keeps the site’s three tooltips and their styling: a poll dot names its pollster and opens that poll in All polls, an event marker explains itself, and anywhere else on a chart shows that month’s figures. Poll dots come back to every chart that has raw polls behind it.

1 · Hovering a poll The dot grows and rings; the tooltip sits above it, and a click opens the poll. 2 · Hovering an event The marker line darkens; the tooltip gives the full name, the date and the site’s one-line description. 3 · Hovering anywhere else on a chart A guide line snaps to the nearest month and the tooltip lists every series, with its 95% interval where the chart shows one. On touch screens, tap to show it and tap elsewhere to hide it. 4 · Copy and expand buttons 44-pixel targets with an accessible name that says which chart they act on.
