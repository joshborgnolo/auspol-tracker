Primary vote First preferences for the governing party and the main opposition party

No opposition has lost as much of its vote this early as the Coalition has

The Coalition is down 10.6 points since the election, to 21.2%; by this stage the average opposition had gained 1.7. Labor is down 7.8, to 26.8%. Only Whitlam’s government, in 1974, had lost more by now.

Albanese’s net approval is the second lowest of any prime minister at this point, after Whitlam’s

At −24.6 he is 25.3 points below the average prime minister 16 months in. He still leads as preferred PM by 12.0 points, close to the average, and Taylor, at −6.9, rates about as opposition leaders usually do.

The final polls have missed the result by 1.4 points on average, and not always the same way

In 2025 all eleven pollsters understated Labor, by 2.8 points on average; in 2019 all five overstated it. Because misses run both ways, what carries over to today’s figures is their size, not their direction.
