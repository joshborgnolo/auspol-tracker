Albanese still leads as preferred PM, but his net approval has fallen 40 points

He leads Taylor 42–38 and Hanson 51–39 head to head, but his lead over the Coalition leader has shrunk from about 18 points under Ley to about 5 under Taylor. His net approval is down about 40 points since the election, to −25, the lowest of the three.
