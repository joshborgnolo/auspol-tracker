# Seating plan — House of Representatives chamber, 45th Parliament

As printed by the Parliament, dated **31 August 2016** — the first revision of the 45th Parliament's seating plan, prepared for its opening sitting (30 August 2016), so the chamber is exactly as elected at the **2 July 2016** double-dissolution election: no by-election results, defections or renames had yet landed. Malcolm Turnbull's Coalition (76 seats) faces Bill Shorten's Labor (69), with a five-member crossbench. No Wayback Machine capture of the seating-plan PDF exists from 2016–2018 (the CDX index was checked on 25 October 2026), so the file itself is the provenance anchor: an InDesign document created 31 August 2016, footer “Serjeant-at-Arms … 31 August 2016”, sha256 `3f760f02fe75209a087ab7042cf3e70d74cfe51edde761a09d61b046caab0b11`; harvested 25 October 2026. Machine-readable form: `house-seating-plan-2016-08-31.json` (same folder).

**Orientation.** One A4 page (the second page is an alphabetical member index with party affiliations). The **Speaker's chair** is at the top, the **table** runs down the centre below it, with the **Clerk, Deputy Clerk and Hansard** at/behind the table (this revision does not print a “THE TABLE” label), and three **Officials'** boxes to the left of the Speaker. The **government** (Coalition, 76) sits on the Speaker's right — the **left-hand side** of the drawing; the **opposition** (69 ALP) on the right-hand side; the five-member **crossbench** (McGowan, Wilkie, Bandt, Katter, Sharkie) wraps around the far curve opposite the Speaker. Seats form **five concentric rows**; the front row's ends reach furthest towards the Speaker.

**Seat order below:** within each row, numbered from the Speaker-end of the government side, around the far curve, to the Speaker-end of the opposition side. The Speaker (Tony Smith) sits in the chair and is listed in row 1 as printed, marked *(chair)*. Parties are as elected in 2016 (XEN = Nick Xenophon Team).
