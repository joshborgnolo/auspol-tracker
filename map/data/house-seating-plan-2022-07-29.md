# Seating plan — House of Representatives chamber, 47th Parliament

As published by the Parliament, dated **29 July 2022** — the first seating plan of the 47th Parliament, recording the chamber as composed at the **2022 federal election**, before any by-elections or party changes. The PDF (created 11 August 2022) is preserved by the Internet Archive at [web.archive.org/web/20220812053437](https://web.archive.org/web/20220812053437/https://www.aph.gov.au/-/media/05_About_Parliament/53_HoR/531_About_HoR/Chamber_seating_plans/Seating_Plan_A4.pdf); harvested 25 October 2026. Machine-readable form: `house-seating-plan-2022-07-29.json` (same folder).

**Orientation.** One A4 page (a second page is a state-by-state summary table). The **Speaker's chair** is at the top, the **table** runs down the centre below it, with the **Clerk, Deputy Clerk and Hansard** at/behind the table, and six **Officials'** boxes flanking the Speaker. The **government** (77 ALP) sits on the speaker's right — the **left-hand side** of the drawing; the **opposition** (Coalition, 58) on the right-hand side; the 16-member **crossbench** wraps around the far curve opposite the Speaker. Seats form **five concentric rows**; the front row's ends reach furthest towards the Speaker.

**Seat order below:** within each row, numbered from the Speaker-end of the government side, around the far curve, to the Speaker-end of the opposition side. The Speaker (Milton Dick) sits in the chair and is listed in row 1 as printed, marked *(chair)*. Parties are as at election night 2022.
