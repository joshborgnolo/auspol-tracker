# Seating plan — House of Representatives chamber, 46th Parliament

As published by the Parliament, dated **March 2021** — the earliest archived revision of the 46th Parliament's seating plan, recording the chamber as it then stood, including the two by-elections held since the **2019 federal election** (Eden-Monaro, 4 July 2020 — won by Labor's Kristy McBain; Groom, 28 November 2020 — won by the LNP's Garth Hamilton). The PDF (created 3 March 2021, footer “Indicative only”) is preserved by the Internet Archive at [web.archive.org/web/20210520030041](https://web.archive.org/web/20210520030041/https://www.aph.gov.au/-/media/05_About_Parliament/53_HoR/531_About_HoR/Chamber_seating_plans/Seating.pdf?la=en&hash=E13361BBF8844D3837FE8EB704FC40238B4C1021); harvested 25 October 2026. Machine-readable form: `house-seating-plan-2021-03.json` (same folder).

**Orientation.** One A4 page (the second page is an alphabetical member index with party affiliations). The **Speaker's chair** is at the top, the **table** runs down the centre below it, with the **Clerk, Deputy Clerk and Hansard** at/behind the table, and six **Officials'** boxes flanking the Speaker. The **government** (Coalition, 77) sits on the Speaker's right — the **left-hand side** of the drawing; the **opposition** (68 ALP) on the right-hand side; the six-member **crossbench** wraps around the far curve opposite the Speaker. Seats form **five concentric rows**; the front row's ends reach furthest towards the Speaker.

**Seat order below:** within each row, numbered from the Speaker-end of the government side, around the far curve, to the Speaker-end of the opposition side. The Speaker (Tony Smith) sits in the chair and is listed in row 1 as printed, marked *(chair)*. Parties are as at March 2021.
