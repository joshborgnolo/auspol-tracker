# Seating plan — House of Representatives chamber, 44th Parliament

As printed by the Parliament, dated **12 November 2013** — the 44th Parliament's **opening day**, so the chamber is exactly as elected at the **7 September 2013** election: no by-election results, defections or renames had yet landed. Tony Abbott's Coalition (90 seats) faces Bill Shorten's Labor (55), with a five-member crossbench. One quirk of this first revision: because the plan was prepared for the sitting that itself elects the Speaker, **no member's label sits at the chair** — Bronwyn Bishop, elected Speaker that day, is listed in the government ranks (row 2). No archived copy of the PDF survives from the era (the Internet Archive holds only a handful of aph.gov.au documents from late 2013, and the seating-plan URL family is first captured in 2019 — CDX index checked 25 October 2026), so the file itself is the provenance anchor: an InDesign CS6 document created 7 November 2013, footer “Serjeant-at-Arms … 12 November 2013”, sha256 `c2b2d6fa24ac1fd2ca852316890acb163b0b763100982b86d9470a0dd195d650`; harvested 25 October 2026. Machine-readable form: `house-seating-plan-2013-11-12.json` (same folder).

**Orientation.** One A4 page (no member-index page). The **Speaker's chair** is at the top, the **table** runs down the centre below it, with the **Clerk, Deputy Clerk and Hansard** at/behind the table (this revision prints neither a “THE TABLE” label nor officials' boxes). The **government** (Coalition, 90) sits on the Speaker's right — the **left-hand side** of the drawing; the **opposition** (55 ALP) on the right-hand side; the five-member **crossbench** (Wilkie, McGowan, Katter, Palmer, Bandt) wraps around the far curve opposite the Speaker. Seats form **five concentric rows**; the front row's ends reach furthest towards the Speaker.

**Seat order below:** within each row, numbered from the Speaker-end of the government side, around the far curve, to the Speaker-end of the opposition side. Parties are as elected in 2013 (PUP = Palmer United Party; the 2013 Coalition figures include Solomon's CLP member).
