Election model (beta)

The 2028 House as the polls stand

Labor would probably win the most seats, probably short of a majority

In 20,000 simulated elections held on the polls to 3 November, Labor wins 65 seats on average (45 to 84 in nine of ten), One Nation 40 (16 to 68) and the Coalition 33 (16 to 51). Labor reaches the 76 seats needed to govern alone in 19% of them.

auspol tracker is an unofficial aggregator of published federal opinion polling.

Best efforts are made to make the aggregate figures transparent, trustworthy, statistically sound, and informative, but they are, in the end, estimates only.

How the figures are built is in Info. Spot an error, a missing poll, or have any other feedback? Please let me know.

Federal polling archives I’ve located are stored here for safekeeping and convenience.
