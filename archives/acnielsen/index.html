AC Nielsen poll archive

The historical AC Nielsen federal poll record, for safekeeping — as published with The Age and The Sydney Morning Herald, 1996–2012, as at the latest aggregate (ALP 51.1 · Coalition 48.9, 31 August 2026).

The AC Nielsen federal poll — annual PDF compilations of voting intention, leadership approval and preferred PM, preserved from au.acnielsen.com via the Internet Archive and mirrored here. No polls were conducted in 2002. Sourced from web.archive.org/web/20060627052718/http://au.acnielsen.com/news/200512.shtml (the 1996–2006 set) and later snapshots of au.acnielsen.com (2007–2012, plus the September 2008 federal vote table).

Before 1996: AGB McNair

Fairfax’s national poll before 1996 was conducted by AGB McNair — the face-to-face precursor of AC Nielsen. Kevin Bonham’s Keating aggregation (1990–1993) streams it alongside Newspoll and Morgan from January 1992. AC Nielsen took over the Fairfax contract in 1996 and switched the series to telephone, so no Nielsen-branded federal poll exists before that year — which is why the archive starts at 1996.

The 71 contemporary newspaper reports below (1990–1995) were located by scanning the full text of the repo’s Trove harvest — every poll-naming newspaper article, 1803–2025 — for the AGB McNair house name. All 71 are from The Canberra Times, the only Fairfax-stable masthead Trove digitised into the 1990s (The Age and the SMH are not in the corpus; their AGB McNair reports are only accessible via those papers’ own archives). Rows with quoted excerpts: agb-mcnair-mentions.csv; vote figures parsed from those same articles: agb-mcnair-figures.csv. Irving Saulwick’s “Age poll” (Saulwick Research) is the sibling Victorian series; it is catalogued under “Saulwick” in the same harvest — see the Trove newspaper archive.
