# Seating plan — House of Representatives chamber, 43rd Parliament

As printed by the Parliament, dated **27 February 2012** — mid-term in the **hung 43rd Parliament** and the **earliest seating-plan revision known to survive in any archive**. Older revisions did exist — the chamber Seating Plan page (first captured 21 February 2012, Wayback `20120221004402`) already carried a back-link to *“seating plan, 24 November 2011”* (`seating_241111.ashx`), the revision drawn the day Slipper took the chair — but no crawl of that or any earlier revision was ever captured: the Internet Archive's CDX index holds nothing before February 2012 anywhere in the `~/media/05 About Parliament/.../Chamber seating plans/` family, on the Seating-Plan page URL, the chamber-documents index (first captured 20 February 2012), or any of the pre-2010 site prefixes probed (`/house/chamber`, `/house/sections`, `/about_parliament/house_of_representatives`, `/documentlibrary`, `/house/practice`, `/house/votes` and a dozen guessed exact URLs, all checked for 2005–2011), and the National Library's Australian Web Archive and PANDORA carry no copy either (all checked 25–26 October 2026). The machine date-stamp of this file — InDesign document created 27 February 2012 — plus that 24 November 2011 back-link are the whole window: the revision series begins no earlier than late 2011, and nothing earlier than this file survives. Julia Gillard's minority Labor government (72) faces Tony Abbott's Coalition (71), with a **six-member crossbench** — any defection moves the chamber — and Peter Slipper, elected as an LNP member in 2010, in the chair as an independent Speaker. The file is Wayback Machine capture `20120228065052` of the old media-library `Seating.ashx` (sha256 `2cfdbd71a71be856768b0515ceb448bd05248ad3fabeaabd7e92a38d7f18a2b3`); InDesign CS3 document created 27 February 2012, footer “Serjeant-at-Arms … 27 February 2012”; harvested 25 October 2026. Machine-readable form: `house-seating-plan-2012-02-27.json` (same folder).

**Orientation.** One A4 page (no member-index page). The **Speaker's chair** is at the top — Slipper's label (“Peter Slipper MP” / “Fisher”) printed directly beneath it; the **table** runs down the centre below it, with the **Clerk, Deputy Clerk and Hansard** at/behind the table (this revision prints neither a “THE TABLE” label nor officials' boxes). The **government** (ALP, 72) sits on the Speaker's right — the **left-hand side** of the drawing; the **opposition** (Coalition, 71) on the right-hand side; the **crossbench** (Windsor, Katter, Oakeshott, Wilkie, Crook, Bandt) wraps around the far curve opposite the Speaker. Seats form **five concentric rows**; the front row's ends reach furthest towards the Speaker.

**Seat order below:** within each row, numbered from the Speaker-end of the government side, around the far curve, to the Speaker-end of the opposition side. Parties are as at 27 February 2012 (LNQ = Liberal National Party of Queensland; KAP = Katter's Australian Party, registered September 2011 — Katter was elected as an independent; the Coalition's NP figure includes Tony Crook, elected as a WA National but sitting on the crossbench).
