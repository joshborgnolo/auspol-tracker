# Seating plan — House of Representatives chamber, 48th Parliament

As published by the Parliament, dated **13 August 2026** [(PDF)](https://www.aph.gov.au/-/media/05_About_Parliament/53_HoR/531_About_HoR/Chamber_seating_plans/Seating_Plan.pdf); harvested 25 October 2026. Machine-readable form: `house-seating-plan-2026-08-13.json` (same folder).

**Orientation.** One A4 page. The **Speaker's chair** is at the top, the **table** runs down the centre of the chamber below it, with the **Clerk, Deputy Clerk and Hansard** at/behind the table, and six **Officials'** boxes flanking the Speaker. The **government** (94 ALP) sits on the speaker's right — the **left-hand side** of the drawing; the **opposition** (Coalition) on the right-hand side; the **crossbench** wraps around the far curve opposite the Speaker. Seats form **five concentric rows**; the front row's ends reach furthest towards the Speaker.

**Seat order below:** within each row, numbered from the Speaker-end of the government side, around the far curve, to the Speaker-end of the opposition side. The Speaker (Milton Dick) sits in the chair and is listed in row 1 as printed, marked *(chair)*.
