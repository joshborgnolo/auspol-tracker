About the archive

There is one row per published wave – never modelled or interpolated – and month rows average the waves whose fieldwork closed within them. A “—” in any column means the pollster didn’t ask that question. Primary votes, two-party figures, leadership ratings and each poll’s links to its source release are all carried on the row.

Sources

Resolve, RedBridge/Accent, DemosAU, Freshwater, Roy Morgan (SMS), Newspoll, Wolf & Smith, YouGov (MRP). Field dates and sample sizes are listed per poll in the archive.
